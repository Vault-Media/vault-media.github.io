Reporting a problem

Include your app version, your device model and what happened. A screenshot helps. Please don't send passwords, sign-in codes or collection details you'd rather keep private.

Deleting your account

Use Delete Account in the app to remove your account and its cloud data. If you can't sign in, email support and we'll help; we may need to confirm you own the account. The privacy policy explains what deletion covers and which shared product data remains.
